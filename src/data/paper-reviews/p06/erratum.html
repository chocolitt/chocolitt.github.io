These errata refer to the version published in Algebraic Geometry 11 (2024), no. 2, 290--295, \href{https://doi.org/10.14231/AG-2024-009} {doi:10.14231/AG-2024-009}. Page and statement references below are to that version.

  1. Page 294, proof of Proposition 2.4, first paragraph. The degree of the final map in the construction is misstated. Replace the sentence beginning “To conclude the proof” by:

    To conclude the proof, it is enough to show that \(\mathscr E_0\) is isotrivial and \(\mathscr X\to\mathscr C_2\) is finite \'etale of degree (2).

    Indeed, \(\mathscr X\to\mathscr C_2\) is obtained by pulling back the double cover

    \[ \mathscr E_0\longrightarrow \mathbb P\bigl(q_*\mathscr O_{\mathscr E}(2t_5)\bigr). \]

    The following sentence already gives the correct calculation: (h) is the composite of maps of degrees (2), (9), and (2), and therefore has degree (36). Proposition 2.4, Remark 2.5, the proof of Theorem 1.2, and the abstract all concern the degree of this composite, so their statements and the later conclusions are unchanged.
