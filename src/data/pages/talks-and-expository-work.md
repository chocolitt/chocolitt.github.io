Below are some notes and slides from such talks.</p><ul><li><p><a href="/s/graphs-sl2.pdf">Graphs and representation theory</a> -- a talk using Lie algebra representation theory to prove some facts from graph theory. The talk is aimed at undergraduate math majors.</p></li><li><p><a href="/s/musicofthespheres.pdf">The music of the spheres </a>-- a talk about the history of (mostly incorrect) theories of cosmology, from Pythagoras to Kepler.
