<p>This course is about commutative algebra, loosely following the textbook of Atiyah-Macdonald. Homework will be due weekly on Thursdays, starting Thursday January 20. Office hours will take place Tuesdays after class, in my office, Boyd 321C.</p><p>The course meets from 1245pm-2pm in Poultry Sciences 0238.</p><p>HW 1 (due Jan 20): Read AM Chapter 1, do exercises 1, 8, 10, 13, 15, 16, and 19.</p><p>HW 2 (due Jan 27): Read AM Chapter 2, do exercises 1, 2, 9, 11, 14, 16, and 20</p><p>HW 3 (due Feb 3): Read Chapter 3, exercises 1, 4, 12. Show that \(\mathbb{Q}\) is a flat \(\mathbb{Z}\)-module which is not free. Prove that if \(B, C\) are \(A\)-algebras, the tensor product algebra \(B\otimes_A C\) has the following universal property: an algebra homomorphism \(B\otimes_A C\to S\) is the same as a pair of algebra homomorphisms \(B\to S, C\to S\).</p><p>HW 4 (due Feb 10): </p><ol><li><p>Let \(f: M\to N\) be a map of modules over a local ring \(A\), with \(N\) finitely generated. Show that if the induced map \(M/\mathfrak{m}M\to N/\mathfrak{m}N\) is surjective, the same is true for \(f\). Does a similar statement hold for injectivity?</p></li><li><p>Let \(M\) be a finitely-generated module over a ring \(A\), and let \(\mathfrak{p}\) be a prime ideal of \(A\). Suppose \(M_\mathfrak{p}=0\). Show that there exists a finite set \(x_1, \cdots, x_n\) of elements of \(A\setminus \mathfrak{p}\) such that the localization of \(M\) at the multiplicative set generated by the \(x_i\) is zero.</p></li><li><p>Let \(M\) be a finitely-generated module over a Noetherian ring \(A\) (that means any submodule of a finitely-generated module is finitely generated), and let \(\mathfrak{p}\) be a prime ideal of \(A\). Suppose \(M_\mathfrak{p}\) is free. Show that there exists a finite set \(x_1, \cdots, x_n\) of elements of \(A\setminus \mathfrak{p}\) such that the localization of \(M\) at the multiplicative set generated by the \(x_i\) is free.</p></li><li><p>Give an example of a module \(M\) over a ring \(A\) such that \(M_\mathfrak{p}\) is free for each prime ideal \(\mathfrak{p}\) of \(A\), but \(M\) itself is not free. Such modules are called <em>locally free</em>.</p></li><li><p>Give an example of a flat module which is not projective.</p></li><li><p>Write a careful proof of the Cayley-Hamilton theorem over an arbitrary field.</p></li></ol><p>HW 5 (due Feb 17): AM Chapter 2, exercises 24, 25, 26</p><ol><li><p>Let \(M\) be an \(A\)-module and let \(F_\bullet: \cdots\to F_2\to F_1\to F_0\) be a flat resolution of \(M\), i.e. a chain complex with each \(F_i\) flat, and such that \(H_i(F_\bullet)=0\) for \(i&gt;0\) and \(H_0(F_\bullet)=M\). Show that for any \(A\)-module \(N\), \(H_i(F_\bullet\otimes_A N)=\operatorname{Tor}_i^A(M, N)\).</p></li><li><p>Let \(M\) be a finitely-generated flat module over a Noetherian local ring \(A\). Show that \(M\) is free.</p></li><li><p>Carefully check that Ext is well-defined, i.e. independent of the choice of injective resolution in the definition.</p></li><li><p>Compute \(\operatorname{Ext}^i_\mathbb{Z}(\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{Z})\) for each prime \(p\).</p></li></ol><p>HW 6 (due Feb 24): AM Chapter 5, exercises 1, 2, 10, 12, 14, and Chapter 6, exercises 2 and 5</p><p>HW 7 (due Thursday, March 17, after Spring Break):</p><ol><li><p>Let \(M\) be an \(A\)-module, and \(f\in A\). Construct an isomorphism between \(M_f\) and \(\varinjlim M\overset{\cdot f}{\to} M\overset{\cdot f}{\to} M \overset{\cdot f}{\to} \cdots\).</p></li><li><p>Construct a module \(M\) over a ring \(A\) such that for each prime ideal \(\mathfrak{p}\) of \(A\), \(M_{\mathfrak{p}}\) is finitely generated, but \(M\) is not finitely-generated.</p></li><li><p>Let \(M\) be a finitely-generated module over a Noetherian ring. Show that \(M=0\) if and only if the support of \(M\) is empty.</p></li><li><p>Suppose \(\text{Spec}(A)=V_1\sqcup V_2 \), where \(V_1, V_2 \) are clopen disjoint subsets. Show that there exists a direct sum decomposition \(A=A_1\oplus A_2\) such that the natural quotient maps \(A\to A_i\) induce isomorphisms \(\text{Spec}(A_i)\to V_i\) for \(i=1,2\).</p></li><li><p>Show that exactness of a long exact sequence is a local property.</p></li><li><p>Let \(A\) be a Noetherian local domain with residue field \(k\) and fraction field \(K\), and \(M\) a finitely-generated \(A\)-module. Show that the following are equivalent:</p><ol><li><p>\(M\) is free</p></li><li><p>\(\text{dim}_k M\otimes_A k=\text{dim}_K M\otimes_A K\).</p></li></ol></li><li><p>Let \(A\) be a Noetherian ring and \(M\) finitely-generated. Show that the following are equivalent:</p><ol><li><p>\(M\) is locally free</p></li><li><p>\(M\) is projective</p></li><li><p>\(M\) is flat.</p></li></ol></li><li><p>Show that any Artinian ring is Noetherian</p></li><li><p>Show that if \(A\) is a Noetherian ring such that \(\text{Spec}(A)\) is Hausdorff, then \(A\) is Artinian.</p></li></ol><p>HW 8 (due March 31)</p><ol><li><p>Give an example (with proof) of a rank one locally free module over a Dedekind domain that is not free.</p></li><li><p>Give an example (with proof) of a Noetherian domain of Krull dimension one which is not a Dedekind domain.</p></li><li><p>Let \(M\) be a finitely generated module over a Dedekind domain \(A\). Show that \(M\) has a projective resolution of length two. Conclude that \(\text{Tor}_i^A(M, -)\) and \(\text{Ext}^i_A(M, -)\) equal zero for \(i&gt;1\).</p></li><li><p>Let \(M,N\) be a finitely generated modules over a Dedekind domain \(A\). Show that \(\text{Tor}_1^A(M,N)\) is a torsion \(A\)-module. Can you identify its support?</p></li><li><p>Give an example of a Dedekind domain with uncountable Picard group.</p></li><li><p>Let \(k\) be a field. Show that the Picard group of \(k[t]\) is trivial, i.e. any rank one locally free module over \(k[t]\) is free.</p></li><li><p>Give an example of a domain with a maximal non-zero ideal \(\mathfrak{m}\) such that \(\mathfrak{m}^2=\mathfrak{m}\).</p></li></ol><p>HW 9 (due by the end of the semester)</p><ol><li><p>Give an example (with proof) of a finitely generated \(k\)-algebra \(A\) which is an integral domain, such that \(\Omega_{A/k}\) is not locally free.</p></li><li><p>Let \(A\to B\to C\) be maps of commutative \(k\)-algebras. Show that we have an exact sequence $$C\otimes \Omega_{B/A}\to \Omega_{C/A}\to \Omega_{C/B}\to 0.$$</p></li><li><p>Compute the Hilbert dimension of \(k[x,y]/(y^2-x^3)\) using the definition.</p></li><li><p>Suppose you have three inverse systems \(A_n, B_n, C_n\), and short exact sequences of inverse systems $$0\to A_n\to B_n\to C_n\to 0.$$ Show that there is a natural exact sequence $$0\to \varprojlim_n A_n\to \varprojlim_n B_n\to \varprojlim_n C_n.$$ Give an example where this sequence is not right exact.</p></li><li><p>Check carefully that the Kahler differentials of a polynomial ring in \(n\) variables are free of rank \(n\).</p></li><li><p>Give an example of a surjection of \(k\)-algebra \(A\to B\) with kernel \(I\), where the natural map \(I/I^2\to \Omega_{A/k}\otimes B\) discussed in class is not injective.</p></li><li><p>Let \(L/k\) be a field extension of finite degree. Show that \(L/k\) is separable if and only if \(\Omega_{L/k}\) is zero.</p></li></ol>
