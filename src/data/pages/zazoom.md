<p>This seminar broadly follow Zannier’s book <strong>Some Problems of Unlikely Intersections in Arithmetic and Geometry</strong>, though we’ll use some other references. You can access it online for free through the UGA library.</p><p>The seminar will take place on Fridays at 4pm Eastern; please let me know if you need the Zoom link. There is also a seminar mailing list; if you are not a member and would like to be, let me know.</p><h2>References</h2><ol><li><p>Zannier, "Some Problems of Unlikely Intersections in Arithmetic and Geometry" (find on line via UGA Library)</p></li><li><p>Scanlon, "<a href="https://math.berkeley.edu/~scanlon/papers/ominaoscanlon.pdf">O-minimality as an approach to the Andre-Oort conjecture</a>"</p></li><li><p>Pila, "<a href="http://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/pila/ODG.pdf">O-minimality and diophantine geometry</a>"</p></li><li><p>Jones, Wilkie "O-Minimality and Diophantine Geometry"</p></li><li><p>Lou van den Dries, "<a href="https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/978-3-540-76591-2_11.pdf">Tame Topology and o-Minimal Structures</a>"</p></li><li><p>Baker, Ribet "<a href="http://www.numdam.org/article/JTNB_2003__15_1_11_0.pdf">Galois theory and torsion points on curves</a>"</p></li></ol><h2>Talks</h2><ol><li><p>(Borys, 9/4) Problems on unlikely intersections: examples (Zannier, Introduction).
