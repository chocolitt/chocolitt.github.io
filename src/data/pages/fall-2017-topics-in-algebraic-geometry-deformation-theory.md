<h2>deformation theory</h2><h2>course description</h2><p>This course is an introduction to deformation theory, at the level of Hartshorne's book on the subject.   Possible topics would include: Schlesinger's criterion, the Tian-Todorov theorem on deformations of Calabi-Yau manifolds, the cotangent complex, infinitsemial variations of Hodge structure, liftability of K3 surfaces, etc.  Familiarity with algebraic geometry at the level of Harshorne's "Algebraic Geometry" is a sufficient prerequisite.</p><p>The course philosophy will be to alternate between theory (e.g. Schlesinger's criterion) and applications which use that theory.  We will cover a different application each week or two.</p><h2>Time and place</h2><p>11:40am-12:55pm, Room 407 in the Columbia math department, Mondays and Wednesdays.  Some special classes Fridays; see the calendar below.</p><h2>Recommended texts</h2><p><a href="http://bookstore.ams.org/surv-123-s">FGA Explained</a> </p><p><a href="https://math.berkeley.edu/~robin/math274root.pdf">Deformation Theory</a>, Hartshorne</p><p><a href="https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0511279.pdf">SGA 2</a></p><h2>Notes</h2><p><a href="http://math.columbia.edu/~hliu">Henry Liu</a> is taking <a href="http://math.columbia.edu/~hliu/classes/f17-algebraic-geometry.pdf">notes on this course</a>.  Please let either him or me know if you spot any errors.</p><h2>Office hours</h2><p>Office hours by appointment.  I will also hold extra sessions some Fridays; see the calendar below.</p><h2>Calendar</h2><p>Wednesday 9/6 - Introduction, tangent-obstruction theories</p><p>Monday 9/11 - Examples: deformation of line bundles, application to Lefschetz theorem for Picard groups, deformations of representations</p><p>Wednesday 9/13 - Deformation rings, Schlessinger's criterion</p><p>Friday 9/15 (room 528, 1-2pm) - Schlessinger's criterion continued, dimensions of deformation rings</p><p>Monday 9/18 - NO CLASS</p><p>Wednesday 9/20 - NO CLASS</p><p>Monday 9/25 - Smoothness of \(\text{Hilb}^n(S)\) for \(S\) a surface</p><p>Wednesday 9/27 -Smoothness of \(\text{Hilb}^n(S)\) for \(S\) a surface, continued; intro to Tian-Todorov</p><p>Monday 10/2 - Deformations of smooth varieties; pro-representability.
