<h2 id="assignments">Assignments</h2>

There will be 5 assignments. The assignment with the lowest grade will be dropped. Late submissions receive a mark of zero unless an extension is granted by the instructor. Tentative due dates are listed in the lecture schedule below.

<h2 id="test">Term Test</h2>

There will be one term test, which is **1.5 hours long**, on **February 13** in class.

<h2 id="final">Final Assessment</h2>

The final assessment will be held during the final assessment period in April 2024, scheduled by the registrar.

<h2 id="schedule">Rough Schedule of Lectures</h2>

| Week | Dates | Topics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Week 1 | Jan 6 - Jan 10 | Galois theory, field extensions |
| Week 2 | Jan 13 - Jan 17 | Algebraic closures, algebraic extensions, splitting fields, normal extensions, **HW 1 due** |
| Week 3 | Jan 20 - Jan 24 | Normal closures, separability, perfect fields, Frobenius, separable degree |
| Week 4 | Jan 27 - Jan 31 | Primitive element theorem, fundamental theorem of Galois theory, examples, **HW 2 due** |
| Week 5 | Feb 3 - Feb 7 | Finite fields, radical extensions, solvability by radicals |
| Week 6 | Feb 10 - Feb 14 | Unsolvability of the quintic, **Term Test (Feb 13)** |
| Week 7 | Feb 17 - Feb 21 | Reading week (no classes) |
| Week 8 | Feb 24 - Feb 28 | Noetherianity, Hilbert basis theorem, finite/finite type extensions, **HW 3 due** |
| Week 9 | Mar 3 - Mar 7 | Zariski’s lemma, Nullstellensatz, Spec and Zariski topology |
| Week 10 | Mar 10 - Mar 14 | Irreducible components, minimal primes, Nakayama lemma, **HW 4 due** |
| Week 11 | Mar 17 - Mar 21 | Semisimple modules and rings, Artin-Wedderburn |
| Week 12 | Mar 24 - Mar 28 | Representation theory of finite groups |
| Week 13 | Mar 31 - Apr 4 | More representation theory of finite groups, **HW 5 due** |
